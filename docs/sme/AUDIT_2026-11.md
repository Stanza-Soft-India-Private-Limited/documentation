## 0. Scope, rules and how to read

**In scope** — everything that talks to our backend, on both sides of the proxy:
- the Vite UI screens that render our data (`portal/admin-ui/src/pages/*`, `components/features/*`);
- the parts of `M:` that call `app.stanzasoft.ai`: `_sme_proxy` (M:4335), `_sme_proxy_raw` (M:6411), `_sme_fetch_all` (M:5524), the reels/video routes (M:3987–4271), `app/services/cms_proxy.py`, `app/services/content_push.py`, and their Python-built `GET /sme/analytics` (M:5625);
- our NestJS `/sme/*` controllers and the keyless legacy routes (`/cms/*`, `/content-doc-admin`, `/reels`, `/videos`, `/reel-blog-admin`).

**Untouchable (AI side)** — §3 is the concrete list: Dify generation (`portal.stanzasoft.ai`), Mux, their auth/RBAC, DynamoDB/S3, in-process jobs. Where an AI flow ends by SAVING to us, the save call is in scope, the generation is not.

**Decisions taken 2026-11-03** (owner): audit first, deployment later; lanes land as `sme-revamp/<lane>` branches + PR on their repo, never merged by us (push to `main` deploys production — §4); only a production portal exists; internal accounts = `@stanzasoft.com`, `@stanzasoft.ai`, `@prepmonkey.*`, `@designasylum.in`, the App Store demo account, sandbox users; prod reconciliation is read-only SQL after the pending backend deploy; PII redacted here, raw captures stay off-repo; the keyless legacy routes ARE scored and get a parity lane (old → new behaviour reproduced end to end).

**Data planes** (why two screens legitimately disagree — detail in `SME_METRICS_DECODER.md`):
- **A domain tables** ("engaged"): attempts, content progress, tasks, psychometric, document progress. Full history.
- **B request capture** ("active"): `api_usage` (purged at 30 days) + `api_usage_daily`. Authenticated requests only, deploy-forward.
- **C billing / user tables.**
engaged < active always; plane-B cards cannot go back past ~30 days whatever window is picked; all buckets IST. Chat, mains-eval and PYQ-variation have no persistence and are invisible in every usage number.

**Verdict scale (Phase 1):** TRUE · MISLABELLED · WRONG · UNKNOWABLE. **Severity:** 🔴 wrong number or data loss · 🟠 misleading · 🟡 missing · ⚪ cosmetic.

### 1.3 Full-scan callers and caps (`_sme_fetch_all`, M:5524–5579)

- Page size 100; `SME_FETCH_MAX_PAGES = 400` (M:5520); `SME_FETCH_CONCURRENCY = 20` (M:5521) → ceiling **40,000 rows** per corpus (was 6,000 in v2.32).
- First page is awaited directly (M:5543): a failure **raises** through the caller. Later pages use `gather(return_exceptions=True)` (M:5557): a failed page is **counted and dropped** (M:5560–5564), rows silently missing.
- `meta = {total, fetched, truncated, failedPages, complete}` (M:5572–5578); `complete = not truncated and failedPages == 0` — it never compares `fetched` with `total`.
- `GET /sme/analytics` coverage (M:5820–5829) = `all(complete)` over users/orders/payments only; the Apple-events leg (max 30 pages = 3,000 rows, M:5644–5646) is reported in `coverage.appleEvents` but **excluded from `coverage.complete`**.
- Callers: `/sme/analytics` ×4 corpora (M:5633–5635, 5644); `/sme/users` with premium/premiumState/date filters (M:4561); `/sme/users/export` (M:4610); `/sme/offers/{id}/performance` → `/sme/orders` (M:7637); bot-volume (M:6108). Other bounded scans: reels titles 10×100 (M:5972–5974), `_count_certain_abandoned` 10×100 (M:7590), answer-mismatches whole PYQ bank (M:8178–8188).
